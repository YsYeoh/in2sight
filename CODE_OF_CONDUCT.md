# Code of conduct

in2Sight is a small project run by volunteers. We want it to be a place where people can ask questions, disagree about technical choices and make mistakes without being treated badly.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Criticise ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome newcomers; explain your reasoning; answer questions kindly.
- Respect privacy. Never share other people's personal data (commenter names, follower lists, tokens, emails) in issues, pull requests, screenshots or chat.
- Disclose AI assistance when you use it, and take responsibility for what you submit.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks of any kind.
- Sexualised language or imagery, threats, doxxing, or publishing someone's private information.
- Spam, or using the project to promote unrelated products.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and may block people who break these rules, at their discretion. To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through the contact details on their GitHub profile (for a security vulnerability, use the private route in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) instead). Reports are treated confidentially.

This code of conduct is deliberately short; its spirit follows the widely used Contributor Covenant.
